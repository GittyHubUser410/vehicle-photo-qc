## 3.0 Rollback evidence

Rehearse migration and restore on disposable data. Keep a complete pre-upgrade backup and matching app checkpoint. Do not run old code against a schema-4 database. Restoring an older snapshot after new writes loses those writes: preserve the upgraded database/files for recovery, stop and reconcile rather than promise an in-place downgrade. No production restore is authorized by this handoff.

## 4.0 Human Test Packet — after Work verification

**Time:** 15–30 minutes. **Setup:** disposable sample gallery, known-good app checkpoint, at least one legacy fixture and deterministic predicted-result fixture; desktop plus Android where available.

1. Open a new training upload: Good defaults remain, but labels show Suggested and not yet exportable.
2. Confirm selected labels, correct one, and use Verify + next: confirm actor and state are understandable without repetitive per-dropdown approvals.
3. Export a mix of verified and unverified records: see which were included and why others were excluded.
4. Inspect a model prediction beside a human label: distinguish confidence/model provenance from verification.
5. Inspect completed, unavailable, not-applicable, legacy, and stale check states: high score must not suggest unchecked work passed.
6. Paste settings and open an unresolved issue on a training-approved photo: verify copying needs review and training approval does not resolve operational work.

Capture screenshots and exact step on failure; do not collect private photos in repository evidence. Record acceptance or specific corrections. Work resolves internal defects before requesting another owner session. Human acceptance is not yet completed.
